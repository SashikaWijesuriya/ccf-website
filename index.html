About the festival

A turning point in
Sri Lankan chess

The Colombo International Chess Festival is the first and longest-running ELO-rated tournament series affiliated with the Chess Federation of Sri Lanka, held since 2008. It is an open event played on the Swiss system, so grandmasters, rising juniors, club players and teams all compete in the same field.

Many of the ELO-rated events now held across the country followed the path the festival started. For many Sri Lankan players, especially from Colombo, Gampaha and Kalutara, the festival has become part of the chess culture.

The 14th edition in 2023, held at the Sugathadasa Indoor Stadium in Colombo, offered a prize pool of LKR 1.3 million, the biggest in the festival's history. In 2026 we plan to build on that.

Chief organiser

Luxman Wijesuriya

Chairman, Organising Committee

Mr Luxman Wijesuriya is one of Sri Lanka’s best-known chess figures. He served as President of the Chess Federation of Sri Lanka for the past 10 years and has supported Sri Lankan chess as an official for nearly 25 years. He is also a Vice President of the Asian Chess Federation.

As a player, he won the Sri Lankan National Championship in 1996 and 2000 and earned medals at Asian and South Asian competitions.

As an administrator, Mr Wijesuriya has worked to strengthen the integrity of chess at the national level. Under his leadership, the Chess Federation of Sri Lanka rose to become one of the country’s top five sports federations, while Sri Lanka came to have the second-highest number of registered chess players in Asia.

Regulations

Tournament rules

Based on the 2023 regulations. Final regulations will be confirmed before the event.

Rules & pairings
  1. FIDE rules and regulations apply. Results will be sent for FIDE rating calculation.
  2. Pairings are made with the Swiss Manager program.
  3. Time control: 90 minutes per player with a 30-second increment per move, from move one.
  4. Tie-breaks will be announced at the technical meeting.
  5. An Appeals Committee will be selected before the tournament begins.
  6. In the event of a dispute, the Chief Arbiter's decision is final.
Conduct in the playing hall
  1. The Chief Arbiter will make an announcement five minutes before each round. Default time is 1 hour; a player who arrives at the board after the default time loses the game.
  2. Mobile phones and other electronic devices are not allowed in the tournament hall. Any violation results in loss of the game.
  3. Officials and players must be smartly dressed at all times in the tournament hall. Slippers and inappropriate clothing are not permitted.
Registration & prizes
  1. All local players must hold a valid CFSL registration.
  2. Organisers reserve the right to accept or reject late registrations.
  3. All amounts are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).
  4. A player can claim only one prize in each tournament. Prize money will be shared.
  5. All prize winners must attend the prize giving in smart casual dress.
Accommodation

GMs, WGMs, IMs and WIMs rated above 2300 will be provided accommodation. Other players can contact the organisers for suitable arrangements.
